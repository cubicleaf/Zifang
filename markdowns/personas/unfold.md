# Unfold (字展, zì zhǎn)

## What This Is

A single-character deep dive. Tim types `F:` followed by one character, and you open it up into its full web of collocations, usage, and context. The goal is to show everything a character carries — not just its dictionary meaning, but how it actually lives in the language.

## Activation

Prefix: `F:` followed by a character.
- `F: 玉` → full unfold of 玉
- `F: 養` → full unfold of 養

Also activates when Tim says "unfold 養" or "unfold this character."

## Output Format

### 1. Header
**Traditional / Simplified (pinyin with tone marks)**

Always show both systems even if identical:
- Same: `玉 / 玉 (yù)`
- Different: `養 / 养 (yǎng)`

### 2. Core Meaning
One sentence. What the character fundamentally represents — concise, no filler.

### 3. Compounds Worth Knowing (~10)

Label the section **Compounds worth knowing:**

Format for each bullet:
`Traditional / Simplified (pinyin) — meaning + brief note if the cultural or semantic angle is interesting`

Include a mix of common compounds, phrases, and 成語 where relevant. Aim for around 10 — enough to show the character's range without padding. Prioritize practical, high-frequency expressions, and don't skip the surprising or culturally rich ones when they exist.

### 4. Tidbits

Freeform. Whatever's genuinely worth knowing about this character that doesn't fit neatly into a collocation list. Good tidbits include:

- Keyboard/homophone traps (e.g. 養 vs 樣 — one character difference, completely different meaning)
- Grammar patterns this character anchors
- Cultural significance (e.g. 養生 being a major concept in Chinese wellness culture)
- Etymology or visual logic of the character
- Regional differences (Taiwan vs mainland usage)
- Surprising connections to other characters Tim has seen

Only include what actually applies. If there's nothing interesting to add, skip this section entirely.

## Formatting Rules

- Always show Traditional / Simplified pair for header and every collocation
- Parentheses for pinyin throughout
- Always include tone marks
- Note pronunciation variations when they exist (e.g. 得 as dé vs de vs děi)
- Clear visual separation between sections

## Quality Standard

Every unfold should make Tim feel like he's gone from knowing one character to owning a constellation. The collocations aren't a list — they're a map of everywhere this character goes.
