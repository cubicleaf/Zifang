# 成语 & 俗语 Collection

**Last updated:** 2026-03-03

---

## The Distinction

### 成语 (chéngyǔ) — Four-Character Idioms
- Almost always exactly **four characters** (四字成语)
- Usually rooted in classical Chinese literature, history, or philosophy
- Compact, literary, often parallel structure
- Used across all registers — formal writing, casual speech, texting
- Example: 叶公好龙 (Yè Gōng hào lóng) — Lord Ye loves dragons (poseur who can't handle the real thing)

### 俗语 (súyǔ) — Folk Sayings / Proverbs
- **Variable length** — can be 5, 6, 7+ characters, or even full sentences
- Colloquial, passed down orally, everyday wisdom
- Less literary, more street-level
- Often humorous or blunt
- Example: 吃鸡蛋挑骨头 (chī jīdàn tiāo gǔtou) — eating eggs and picking out bones (being nitpicky where there's nothing to find fault with)

### Key Difference
成语 = literary, four characters, classical roots.
俗语 = colloquial, any length, folk roots.

Both are idiomatic. English "idiom" covers both, but Chinese distinguishes them. Vian corrected Tim on this: 吃鸡蛋挑骨头 is 俗语, not 成语.

| 成语 | Pinyin | Meaning | Context |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 因人施教 | yīn rén shī jiào | teach according to the individual | Attributed to 孔子. Vian used this to compliment Tim. From the Analerta: 子路 and 冉有 received opposite advice from Confucius on the same question. |
| 叶公好龙 | Yè Gōng hào lóng | Lord Ye loves dragons (poseur) | Claims to love something, runs from the real thing. Note: 好 = hào (4th tone, "fond of"), NOT hǎo. |

## 俗语 (Folk Sayings / Proverbs)

| 俗语 | Pinyin | Meaning | Context |
|------|--------|---------|---------|
| 吃鸡蛋挑骨头 | chī jīdàn tiāo gǔtou | eating eggs, picking out bones | Being nitpicky where there's nothing to fault. Tim used this thinking it was 成语 — Vian corrected him. 5 characters = 俗语, not 成语. |
| 还给老师 | huán gěi lǎoshī | returned it to the teacher | Forgot everything learned in school. Knowledge as a loan you failed to keep. Vian used: 希望没有都还给老师. Cultural: implies school taught you, but you let it slip. |
| 棺材板都压不住了 | guāncai bǎn dōu yā bú zhù le | even the coffin lid can't hold him down | Rolling in one's grave. Internet-era expression. |
| 强扭的瓜不甜 | qiáng niǔ de guā bù tián | a melon twisted off by force isn't sweet | Can't force a good thing. Applied to Vian's WhatsApp vibe shift. |
