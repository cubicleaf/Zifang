# HSK 5 — Scout A: Trad/Simp & Taiwan-vs-Mainland Hazard Map

**Source:** `/Users/cubicleaf/Documents/webdev/Chinese shit/data/hsk5-source-2012.txt` (1,300 words, Hanban Sept 2012, simplified, one-per-line).
**Mission:** Surface conversion traps for Trad-first / Taiwan-modern build. Precedent from HSK 4: 台 not 臺, 聯絡 not 聯繫, 支持 not 支援, 通過 not 透過.
**Scope:** Hazards only — no card generation. Items absent from the HSK 5 list are NOT discussed (e.g. 出租车 / 計程車 is not in HSK 5; 凤梨/菠萝 not in list; 网络 IS in list as line 969).

## Section 1 — High-stakes Trad/Simp pairs

These are entries where blind OpenCC-style mechanical conversion will produce a form that is either wrong, ambiguous, or jarring to a Taiwanese reader. This is a **character-variant** problem (one Simplified char maps to multiple Traditional chars, or vice versa), distinct from the lexical-substitution problem in Section 2.

## Section 2 — Taiwan-vs-Mainland lexical choices

These are NOT character-variant issues. The Mainland and Taiwan use **different words** (different morphemes). Mechanical conversion of the simplified form yields a "correct" trad form that is the wrong word for Taiwan readers. Recommend overrides per HSK 4 precedent (Tim already established the pattern).
