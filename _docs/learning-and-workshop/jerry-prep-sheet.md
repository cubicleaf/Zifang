## 1. CALL MECHANICS
*Survival phrases for managing the call itself*

---

**Wait, say that again**
```
等一下，你再說一次？
děng yīxià, nǐ zài shuō yīcì?
wait a moment | you | again | say | once
```

---

**I didn't catch that / I didn't understand**
```
我沒聽清楚
wǒ méi tīng qīngchǔ
I | didn't | hear | clearly
```

---

**Let me look that up**
```
我查一下
wǒ chá yīxià
I | look up | a moment
```

---

**How do you say ___ in Chinese?**
```
___用中文怎麼說？
___ yòng Zhōngwén zěnme shuō?
___ | in Chinese | how | say?
```

---

**My Chinese is really bad, sorry**
```
我的中文很爛，對不起
wǒ de Zhōngwén hěn làn, duìbuqǐ
my | Chinese | very | terrible | sorry
```
*(Self-deprecating, disarming — Taiwanese people respond well to this)*

---

**My typing is much better than my speaking**
```
我打字比說話好很多
wǒ dǎzì bǐ shuōhuà hǎo hěnduō
I | type | compared to | speaking | better | a lot
```

---

**I want to practice speaking**
```
我想練習說話
wǒ xiǎng liànxí shuōhuà
I | want | practice | speaking
```

---

**Can you speak a little slower?**
```
你可以說慢一點嗎？
nǐ kěyǐ shuō màn yīdiǎn ma?
you | can | speak | slow | a little | ?
```
